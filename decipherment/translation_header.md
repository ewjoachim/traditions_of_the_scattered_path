# Draft translation

A sentence-by-sentence draft of the booklet, built on [glossary.md](./glossary.md).
Nothing here is confirmed by the author.

How to read it:

- Plain words follow the glossary. Many of those rest on a few contexts only, so check
  the glossary's confidence column before relying on a word.
- **[Bracketed]** words are guesses from context or from the picture: either the word is
  not in the glossary, or its glossary entry is too weak to lean on. Treat them as
  placeholders that fit, not as readings. There are about three per sentence.
- **[...]** marks a stretch that cannot be read yet.
- English word order has been restored; the booklet puts the verb first.
- Names such as Ktbew or Ctvork are the transcript's arbitrary letters, not pronunciations.

Sentences are numbered section.sentence, in the order of the
[transcript](./transcript/joined_text.txt). The drafts are kept in
[decipherment/translations.tsv](./decipherment/translations.tsv), one row per sentence.
